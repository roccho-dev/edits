## UX contract proved

The proof covers a Vim package that keeps the real buffer in romaji while presenting a Japanese preedit projection and mixed completion candidates.

Core behavior:

- raw buffer remains English/romaji while typing
- preedit view projects romaji to hiragana
- normal candidates and Japanese dictionary candidates are both visible
- normal candidates retain higher rank unless the selected lane moves
- selected candidate drives both ghost preview and commit result
- commit is the only buffer-writing boundary
- no popup means Tab remains editor-owned
