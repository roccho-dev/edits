# why this contributes

The direct product value is keeping a small Japanese-aware completion package reusable across editors and hq/modeling sources.

The higher business value is that a small, tested, adapter-based package is easier to review, transfer, price, and maintain than a Vim-only or data-format-coupled implementation.

The highest-purpose contribution is reduced sale/DD risk: behavior, boundaries, and post-merge proof are visible as CI evidence instead of personal knowledge.
